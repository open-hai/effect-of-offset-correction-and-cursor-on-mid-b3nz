## The paper

Mayer, Schwind, Schweigert & Henze, *The Effect of Offset Correction and Cursor on Mid-Air Pointing in Real and Virtual Environments*, CHI '18 ([10.1145/3173574.3174227](https://doi.org/10.1145/3173574.3174227)). Two lab studies: 20 people point at a 7×5 target grid in a real room and a VR replica (8,400 postures), a 15-parameter quartic is fitted to cancel the systematic offset, then 16 new people validate it in a 2×2×2 selection task.

## Verdict: partial

**Boundary.** 15 inner-loop components (ray-cast geometry → offsets → polynomial fits → LOOCV → the ANOVAs and every derived percentage), 7 outer-loop components (the two studies, raw TLX, eye-dominance screening, anthropometry, consent, recruitment) — never attempted, never simulated, never scored.

**Per-component table: 8 verified, 4 partial, 3 blocked.** The rate is stated in the repo only as a derived footnote to the table, explicitly non-comparable across papers or runs.

**What reproduced.** Everything the paper derives from its own tables — all 35 checkable derivations, including the 29.3 % headline correction, the 13.1 % / 4.5 % Study-2 improvements, and the cross-paper ratios against the 2015 predecessor (which also pinned down that the paper's word "overall" there can only mean the RealWorld column). Equation 1's basis reconstructs exactly as the complete bivariate quartic, and the paper's odd "nonlinear least-squares solver" choice is verified harmless (solvers agree to 6.5 × 10⁻¹⁰).

**What blocked.** No data, and — the sharpest finding — **not one of the ~240 fitted coefficients was ever published**, so the paper's stated contribution cannot be applied by a reader. The predecessor paper did print its coefficients; I re-implemented those in `src/mayer2015_model.py` to show what is missing.

**Mismatches.** 6 of 20 reported *F*/*p* pairs fail recomputation, including a main effect the paper calls significant: `ENVIRONMENT` on accuracy, *F*(1,15) = 1.3, *p* = .027, where that *F* gives *p* = .272. Study 2's fatigue ANOVA reports *F*(5,15) where its own design implies *F*(5,75).

**Artifacts.** 22 locations searched and logged. Two footnote artifacts (marker mounts, hand meshes) are alive after eight years; no code, data, license, or preregistration exists. ACM DL and ResearchGate returned HTTP 403 and are recorded as blocked rather than as absence.

The inner loop is implemented and actually run (`./run_all.sh`, output recorded in `results/run_log.txt`); `src/check_paper.py` needs no data at all and is what produced the mismatch findings.
